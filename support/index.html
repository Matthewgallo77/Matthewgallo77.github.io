Quick answers for setting up and using ScrollDoctor on your iPhone.

Quick help

Why does ScrollDoctor request Screen Time access?

Screen Time permission lets Apple’s privacy-preserving system protect the apps and websites you select. ScrollDoctor cannot read your feeds, messages, or browsing.

What kinds of protection can I use?

Start free with one app, one website, and one weekly schedule for each. Pro adds more apps, websites, schedules, and permanent blocking.

How do I change my protected apps or websites?

Settings are locked while protection is on. Turn protection off using the local waiting period and four-digit passcode, make your changes, then turn it back on.

What happens when I reach a daily limit?

ScrollDoctor asks you to close the app or website. If you continue, the reminder returns at increasingly shorter usage intervals.

What is Quick Relief?

Open ScrollDoctor and tap Quick Relief on an eligible blocked app or website, then enter your protection passcode. You get 15, then 5, then 3 minutes across all protections each day. Protection returns automatically.

Does ScrollDoctor see what I select or view?

Apple gives ScrollDoctor privacy-preserving selection tokens. Your Screen Time information is processed locally, and ScrollDoctor does not inspect what you view or send it to us.

How do I remove my information?

Remove configured protections in ScrollDoctor and manage Screen Time permission in iOS Settings. ScrollDoctor does not maintain an account or server copy of your information.

Still need help?

Email scrolldoctorapp@gmail.com with your iOS version, ScrollDoctor version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passcodes or other sensitive information.
