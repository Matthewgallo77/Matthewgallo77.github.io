Quick help

Why does ScrollDoctor request Screen Time access?

Screen Time permission lets Apple’s privacy-preserving system measure the apps you select and show a shield when you reach your limit. ScrollDoctor cannot read your feeds or messages.

Why can’t I change my apps or limit?

Selections and limits are locked while protection is on. Turn protection off using the local waiting period and four-digit passcode, make your changes, and turn it back on.

Why did a reminder return after I continued?

That is the core protection behavior. After you pass your limit and choose to continue, ScrollDoctor brings the reminder back at increasingly shorter usage intervals.

Does ScrollDoctor see which apps I select?

Apple gives ScrollDoctor privacy-preserving selection tokens. Your Screen Time information is processed locally and is not sent to us.

How do I remove my information?

ScrollDoctor does not maintain an account or server copy of your information. You can manage Screen Time permission in iOS Settings and delete the app to remove its locally stored data.
