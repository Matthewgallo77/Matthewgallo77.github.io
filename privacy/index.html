Privacy Policy

ScrollDoctor is designed to work locally on your iPhone without collecting your personal data.

Effective August 19, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ScrollDoctor, published by Matthew Gallo (“ScrollDoctor,” “we,” or “us”), handles information when you use the ScrollDoctor iPhone app and this website.

In short: ScrollDoctor does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data from the app. Screen Time processing and protection settings remain on your device.

Information handled by the app

ScrollDoctor uses Apple’s Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks to provide its core features. With your permission, these Apple systems allow the app to:

  • Store privacy-preserving tokens for apps you select.
  • Measure aggregate usage for those selections on your device.
  • Apply Apple-provided Screen Time shields after a daily limit.
  • Store your limit, protection state, timing stage, and related settings locally.
  • Store a salted one-way digest of your protection passcode in the iOS Keychain.

The app and its extensions may cache a same-day aggregate usage value using a hashed, opaque identifier so local reminders can work. This cache does not contain a readable app name or detailed activity history, and prior-day samples are removed.

Information we do not collect

ScrollDoctor does not operate an app server and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The app does not:

  • Create or require a user account.
  • Read the content of social-media feeds or messages.
  • Access your social-media accounts.
  • Inspect network traffic or install a VPN.
  • Track you across apps or websites.
  • Sell or share personal information.

Your control

You choose whether to grant Screen Time authorization and which apps to protect. You can turn protection off inside ScrollDoctor after completing its local passcode flow. You can also manage Screen Time permission in iOS Settings or delete ScrollDoctor to remove its locally stored app data.

This website

This website does not use advertising, analytics, cookies, contact forms, or tracking scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process basic technical information when delivering the site under its own privacy statement.

Children’s privacy

ScrollDoctor does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if ScrollDoctor’s features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email mattthewgallo@gmail.com or visit the ScrollDoctor Support page. Because ScrollDoctor does not collect app data or maintain user accounts, there is no server-held personal data to access, export, or delete.
